# AI Prompt Engineering & Business Intelligence

A practical portfolio demonstrating how **Prompt Engineering, Generative AI, and AI-assisted research** can be applied to real-world business problems.

This repository documents the development of structured prompts, advanced prompting techniques, business analysis frameworks, research workflows, source verification, and AI-assisted decision support.

The projects focus on moving beyond simple AI queries toward **structured, reliable, and business-oriented AI workflows**.

---

## Overview

This portfolio demonstrates how AI can support different stages of business analysis and decision-making, including:

* Marketing analysis
* Human Resource analysis
* Financial analysis
* Operations and process improvement
* Customer service analysis
* Business strategy
* Product and market analysis
* AI-assisted research
* Source verification and citation
* Decision support

The repository also includes a detailed **PhonePe case study** demonstrating how prompt refinement and AI-assisted research can be applied to a real company.

---

## Core Skills Demonstrated

### Prompt Engineering

The portfolio covers multiple prompting approaches, including:

* RCTF Prompting
* Constraint-based prompting
* Few-shot prompting
* Structured prompting
* Prompt chaining
* Iterative prompt refinement
* AI self-critique
* Hallucination prevention
* Fact vs. assumption vs. recommendation separation
* Decision-matrix prompting

These techniques are used to improve the **clarity, relevance, structure, and reliability** of AI-generated outputs.

## AI-Assisted Business Research

The research workflow focuses on using AI as a research assistant while maintaining human oversight.

### Research Workflow

**Research Question → AI Research Support → Search → Original Sources → Verification → Evidence Table → Analysis → Citation → Human Review**

The portfolio emphasizes:

* Verifying important claims
* Using original and credible sources
* Avoiding invented statistics and sources
* Identifying outdated or time-sensitive information
* Separating facts from interpretations
* Triangulating information across sources
* Maintaining human review throughout the research process

---

## Featured Case Study: PhonePe

One of the major projects in this repository applies the prompt engineering and research methodology to **PhonePe**.

The project demonstrates the progression from a broad research question to a more structured and source-oriented prompt.

### Prompt Refinement

Instead of relying on a generic question such as:

> "Tell me about AI in PhonePe."

The research process develops increasingly specific prompts that define:

* The research objective
* Required information
* Time period
* Source requirements
* Verification requirements
* Output structure

This demonstrates how **prompt quality directly influences the usefulness of AI-assisted research**.

## Research & Reliability Principle

> **AI can accelerate research, but important information should be verified against reliable original sources before being treated as factual.**

This portfolio therefore combines **AI-generated assistance with source verification and human review**.

## Purpose

The purpose of this repository is to demonstrate the practical application of **Generative AI and Prompt Engineering in business and research environments**.

Rather than focusing only on individual prompts, the portfolio demonstrates how prompts can be designed as part of a broader workflow for:

**Research → Analysis → Verification → Decision Support**
